<!-- ZH -->
手势旋转
中指，无名指，小拇指握拳并且手掌向右垂直，进入旋转模式手变成高亮状态
保持高亮状态，捏合食指和大拇指，触发向右旋转（左手同理）
食指捏合同样作用物体抓取
<!-- EN -->
Hand-gesture turning
Curl the middle, ring, and little fingers into a fist and hold the palm vertically facing right. The hand becomes highlighted when turning mode is active.
Keep the hand highlighted and pinch the index finger and thumb to turn right. Apply the same gesture with the left hand to turn left.
The index-finger pinch is also used to grab objects.
<!-- /DOCBLOCK -->

<!-- ZH -->
手势传送
右手中指，无名指，小拇指握拳并且手掌向上时，进入传送模式手变成高亮状态，捏合食指和大拇指，触发向传送移动。
<!-- EN -->
Hand-gesture teleportation
With the right palm facing upward, curl the middle, ring, and little fingers into a fist. The hand becomes highlighted when teleportation mode is active. Pinch the index finger and thumb to teleport.
